GOOption C · Hybrid

      

        Final recommendation
        
Build Option C, the hybrid. Each zone node sanitizes its own readings and runs a Z-score plus an int8 autoencoder (~14 KB), so frost and ventilation failures are caught and acted on in about 6.4 s, well inside the 15 s limit, even with no internet. A local Mosquitto broker and a 56 KB-per-node flash ring buffer keep 24 h of telemetry, then burst-sync over TLS with sequence-number de-duplication. The cloud side is a single low-cost VPS running Mosquitto, TimescaleDB and Grafana for history, dashboards and weekly Isolation Forest retraining. Everything uses open protocols and open-source licences, so the exit cost stays low. Next step: a 14-day simulated pilot with 40 injected events, gated on recall ≥ 0.95, ≤ 1 false alarm/day and zero sequence gaps after a 24 h outage.
